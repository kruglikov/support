# Dominion Life — English → Russian glossary

One Russian rendering per concept, used on every page. Status values: `approved` (fixed, use everywhere), `proposed` (use unless changed), `decide` (open question, do not translate pages that contain the term until decided), `context` (inside a Bible quotation the Синодальный перевод word is used; in the author's text the translator picks the rendering that fits the sentence and always adds the English original in brackets at its first occurrence in each chapter). A row changes status only by an explicit decision; when a row changes, every page already translated with the old rendering must be revisited.

Scope: the terms apply to Curry Blake's own text — prose, headings, and the definitions of Strong's entries. Inside a Bible quotation the text of the Синодальный перевод is used unchanged, even where its wording differs from this table; the terms were chosen to agree with the Синодальный перевод wherever possible, so the reader recognises the same word in the author's text and in the verse.

English in brackets: for a term marked `yes` in the column "EN", the English original follows the Russian term in round brackets at its first occurrence in each chapter, for example «владычество (dominion)». Later occurrences in the same chapter are written without brackets. Terms marked `no` never carry the English original.

## 2. Bible quotations

1. Every quotation marked KJV is replaced by the text of the same verse in the Синодальный перевод (Russian Synodal translation, 1876, public domain). The King James text is never translated word for word.
2. The book marks a KJV quotation in two ways: a quoted verse followed by `KJV __`, and a verse-numbered passage headed `<reference> KJV` (for example "Mark 13:31-37 KJV"). The Strong's usage list `KJV - ` is not a quotation and follows section 3 instead.
3. References are written with Russian book names and abbreviations of the Синодальный перевод (table below), for example "Rev 1:4-7" → "Откр. 1:4-7".
4. Verse numbering differs between the KJV and the Синодальный перевод in a few places. The main difference is in Psalms 10–147, which the Синодальный перевод numbers one lower (KJV Psalm 23 = Псалом 22). Every quotation from Psalms, and every reference flagged as a numbering difference, must be checked against the Russian verse before the quotation is inserted.
5. When the author's argument depends on a KJV word the Синодальный перевод does not use (for example Luke 19:13 KJV "Occupy till I come" vs Синодальный перевод «употребляйте их в оборот, пока я возвращусь»), keep the Синодальный перевод text and add a translator's note in square brackets: `[в английском переводе KJV: «occupy» — …]`.
6. Words the author inserts into a quotation in parentheses or square brackets, such as "(authority)", are kept as the author's insertion and translated, placed at the same point of the Russian verse.
8. Version label: wherever the English text carries the marker "KJV" (after the last verse of a passage, or after a reference such as "Mark 13:31-37 KJV"), the Russian text carries «(Синод.)» at the same place, for example «Мк. 13:31-37 (Синод.)».
9. Other Bible versions: none appear in this book (a scan of all 99 pages on 2026-09-29 found only KJV markers). If a later edition quotes another version, translate the quotation from English, mark it with the version abbreviation, and add the Синодальный перевод reference so the reader can compare.

## 3. Strong's dictionary entries

The book quotes 23 entries from Biblesoft's New Exhaustive Strong's Numbers and Concordance, on PDF pages 6, 8, 10, 18, 40, 76 and 95. An entry appears in two shapes: `OT:7287 - radah (raw-daw'); …` and, with the English headword first, `Power - NT:1411 - dunamis (doo'-nam-is); …`. Every entry must read in Russian as a quotation from Strong's concordance (Симфония Стронга), so each entry is rebuilt to one fixed pattern:

`Стронг ВЗ:7287 — radah (радах): <definition>. В KJV переводится как: <usage list>.`

`Сила — Стронг НЗ:1411 — dunamis (дюнамис): <definition>. В KJV переводится как: <usage list>.`

| Part | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Label | the word «Стронг» opens the number, so the source is named in every entry | approved |
| Number | `OT:` → `ВЗ:` (Ветхий Завет), `NT:` → `НЗ:` (Новый Завет); the digits are unchanged; the same replacement applies to numbers cross-referenced inside a definition ("whereas NT:138 …" → «тогда как НЗ:138 …») | approved |
| English headword (`Power - …`) | translated with the Russian term from §1, «Сила — …» | approved |
| Original word | the transliterated Greek or Hebrew word stays in Latin letters, `radah`, `dunamis` | approved |
| Pronunciation | the English respelling `(raw-daw')` is replaced by a Russian transcription `(радах)` | approved |
| Definition | translated into Russian in the dictionary's terse style; "i.e." → «т.е.», "specifically" → «в частности», "by implication" → «в переносном смысле», "figuratively" → «образно» | approved |
| KJV usage list | the English words of the list are translated into Russian and introduced with «В KJV переводится как:», because the author argues from how the King James Version renders the word; the list keeps its original order, and its bracketed variants such as "(come to, make to)" are translated inside the brackets | approved |
| Bold words | a word the author bolds inside an entry stays bold in the Russian entry | approved |

Translator's note, placed once at the first entry of the book (PDF page 6): «Здесь и далее номера слов даны по Симфонии Стронга: ВЗ — Ветхий Завет (еврейские слова), НЗ — Новый Завет (греческие слова). Список «В KJV переводится как» показывает, какими словами это слово передано в английской Библии короля Якова (King James Version).»

The same Strong's number always gets the same Russian definition, even when the book quotes the entry on several pages (NT:1849 appears on PDF pages 8, 10 and 76).
